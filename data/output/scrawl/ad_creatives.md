## How each ad is made

1. **Background image**: drawn by FLUX.2 on Cloudflare from the *image prompt* below. It contains no text, because image models misspell words; the prompt keeps a clean area free for the text.
2. **Finished ad**: code typesets the text below onto that clean area with exact spelling: brand line, headline, subline, CTA button and website. Output: `final_ads/<trend>_<type>_<ratio>_ad.png`.
3. **Post**: upload the finished ad and paste the caption and hashtags.
